## The years, and the four calls behind them

1. **Aristotle, c. 335 BCE.** Our biography doesn't date the Nicomachean Ethics. It does date the Lyceum: "Around 335 BCE ... founded the Lyceum, which he ran for 12 years". So the year marks the Lyceum, and the Ethics is named without a date.
2. **Ellis, 1962, not "the year he founded REBT".** His article gives no single founding year. He'd broken from psychoanalysis "by 1953", the approach was "first called Rational Therapy", it became Rational Emotive Therapy in 1959, and he set out the model in book form in 1962. The book is the one dated event, and the Academy's sources use it too.
3. **Beck, 1967.** Post 35574 says his 1967 book "coined automatic thoughts", which is the idea this moment is about. His first papers came in 1963 and the book that named cognitive therapy in 1976. The Academy already cites both 1967 and 1976.
4. **Bandler and Grinder, "1970s".** Our NLP articles say only that NLP "began in California in the 1970s". The Structure of Magic is dated "mid 1970s" (post 39271). So the year reads "1970s".

Frankl's 1946 is confirmed: "German readers had it first, in 1946" (post 35226), with the English edition in 1959.
