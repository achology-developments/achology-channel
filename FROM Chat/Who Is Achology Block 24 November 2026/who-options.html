Who Is
Achology?

Achology is the Academy of Modern Applied Psychology, and the community of people who learn there. For a century, psychology's most useful ideas stayed inside universities and clinics. We take the ones you can learn from and use, and teach them in everyday words, so you can understand yourself, relate better to others and live a wiser, more responsible life. You study at your own pace, practise with other members at live events every week, and in time pass on what you have learned. Nobody starts as an expert. Everyone starts somewhere.
