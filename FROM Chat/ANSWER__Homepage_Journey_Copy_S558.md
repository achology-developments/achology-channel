## Calls Chat made that Kain may overturn

1. **The professional body isn't named.** Step 4 says "a professional body separate from the courses" and doesn't name SoMAP, which by standing rule appears only as a link.
2. **Every price line says what you pay and what you get,** as the house rule asks. The membership line gives the $7 and then the monthly price, and says nothing about what the trial leaves out (DSRD 4, Kain's 17 November rule).
3. **"Treat" is a Stance Never word,** so the clinical line reads "Achology is education, not clinical care". The published answer says "teaches and does not treat".
4. **"Who it isn't for" has three lines, not two.** The published answers name three plain misfits: guaranteed change, managed learning and clinical need. Each one saves a reader money.
5. **The free list keeps four items,** which is the most the budget allows. It now includes the first six lessons, which the Events and course pages already offer.
