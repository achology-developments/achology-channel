## 1. Paragraph one: what a presupposition is

A presupposition is something you take to be true before you start, usually without noticing you've done it. Every conversation runs on them. Ask a friend when they'll stop worrying, and you've already assumed that they worry. In our courses, and above all in neuro-linguistic programming (NLP), the word means something a little more deliberate: a belief about people that you choose to work from because it's useful, not because it can be proved. "There is no failure, only feedback" is one of the best known. Nobody is saying failure doesn't exist or doesn't cost anything. It's an attitude you hold on purpose, because of what it lets you learn.

## 2. Paragraph two: Achology's own, and how they run through every course

Achology has seven presuppositions of its own. We believe every human being can grow and mature, that self-knowledge comes before personal growth, and that wisdom begins with accurate thinking. We believe emotions can be understood and managed, that your emotional state is your responsibility, and that personal growth comes before a better life and always serves a purpose. These aren't a lesson you study once and leave behind. They sit underneath every course we teach, from life coaching to hypnotherapy, and they're the reason every course asks you to try each idea on your own life first. The articles below explain each one in full.
