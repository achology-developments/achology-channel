# ANSWER: which companies run Achology, and which one a buyer contracts with

## The finding

**There's no clash on the companies.** The pages name three companies, and each does a different job. The sources agree once that's seen.

| Company | Number | What it does | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Achology Transactions Ltd (ATL) | SC697126, Scotland | Trades as Achology. It sells every course, bundle and the Access All Areas Pass, and community subscriptions from 1 June 2023. It's the data controller in the Privacy Policy. | Terms and Conditions ("Who we are", "Your order"); Privacy Policy; Help 10053, 10054, 257, 388; the Policies index banner link |
| The Academy of Modern Applied Psychology Ltd (AMAP) | SC612822, Scotland | Also trades as Achology. It's the company behind community subscriptions bought before 1 June 2023. | Help 10053, 10054, 257, 388 |
| Kain Ramsay Limited | Not published | Not a trading company. It's named only as a group and joint controller of personal data, "where relevant to providing access to courses, events, or related educational services". | Privacy Policy; Help 383 |

Both trading companies share one registered office: Clyde Offices, 2nd Floor, 48 West George Street, Glasgow, G2 1BP (Help 257). Both are owned and run by Kain and Karen Ramsay (Help 273; the Policies index: "through two registered companies in Scotland, UK").

**So the Help answer naming the Academy of Modern Applied Psychology Ltd isn't wrong.** It names AMAP only for subscriptions bought before 1 June 2023, and it names ATL for everything else.

## The one real gap

The Terms and Conditions say "Community subscriptions are likewise contracts with Achology Transactions Ltd (ATL)", with no date. Help 10053 says subscriptions bought before 1 June 2023 belong to AMAP. One of them is incomplete, and only Kain knows which:
- If the old subscriptions still run under AMAP, the Terms need one sentence added.
- If they've all been moved to ATL, Help 10053 needs its AMAP row taken out, and so do 10054, 257 and 388.

**The question for Kain, yes or no:** "Are community subscriptions bought before 1 June 2023 still contracts with The Academy of Modern Applied Psychology Ltd?"
