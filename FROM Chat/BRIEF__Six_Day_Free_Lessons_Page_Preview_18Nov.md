The words in item 1 and on the locked days are working words. Chat writes the final words before Kain signs.

**How a day unlocks.** Each reader gets their own link, so the page knows their day without a login.

- At sign up, the site makes a reader token, stores it with the sign up time and the course or school, and sends Kit the reader's own page address with the token in it (free_lessons_link for courses, free_teachings_link for schools).
- Day 1 opens at sign up. Day n opens (n minus 1) times 24 hours after sign up, the same timing as the Kit emails.
- The page remembers the token in that browser, so a reader who comes back without the link still sees their days.
- Without a token, the page shows day 1 open and the rest locked, with the course's sign up form in place of the day count.
- For the preview, a preview switch (for example `?preview_day=3`, admins only) shows the page as a reader sees it on any day.
