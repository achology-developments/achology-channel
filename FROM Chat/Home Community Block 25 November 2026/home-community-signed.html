Who Is
Achology?

Achology is an academy, a community and a movement of people learning to understand themselves and help others do the same.

For a century, psychology's most useful ideas stayed inside universities and clinics. We take the ones you can learn from and use, and teach them in everyday words, so you can understand yourself, relate better to others and live a wiser, more responsible life.

Achology Is a Collaborative Learning Community

Members study the same courses, practise together at live events and, in time, learn to lead those events themselves.

1 Learn Together

Every weekday, members meet online to talk through what they're studying and try it out with each other. You don't need to know anything first, only to be willing to take part.

2 Learn to Lead

Members who train as hosts run the workshops and discussions. Leading a group builds facilitation skills you only learn by doing, and counts toward your next member level.

3 Serve at the Centre

Accredited members can apply to coach, mentor and run events at The Centre of Human Development, by Achology, a new home for people who want to grow, opening in 2027.

Questions People Ask Before They Join

Straight answers on whether Achology is recognised, what it costs and who it suits, before you decide anything.

Recognition

  • Is Achology accredited?Yes, by SoMAP, whose committee of Master Achologists checks every claim on the accreditation pathway.
  • Are Achology certificates recognised internationally?They record the same training wherever you live; a regulated profession needs its own licence in every country.
  • Will employers recognise my Achology certificate?Employers who judge what you can do may value it; a job that needs a licence needs that profession's own qualification.

Cost and Value

  • Is Achology worth the money?Worth it for practical, community-based learning; not for passive content or quick credentials.
  • Why is Achology priced higher than Udemy but lower than universities?Each is built differently: Achology sits between marketplace content and institutional study.
  • Does Achology offer a free trial or introductory rate?There's no free trial, but free workbooks and lessons cost nothing, and your first 30 days of full Achology Membership cost $7.

Who It's For

  • Who is Achology designed for?Reflective, self-directed adults: helpers, professionals, and people learning for their own growth.
  • Who is Achology not designed for?Not for quick fixes, passive learning, clinical treatment, crisis support, or university credentials.
  • Is Achology suitable for beginners with no background in psychology?Yes: no prerequisites; open-minded beginners can start anywhere in the curriculum.
