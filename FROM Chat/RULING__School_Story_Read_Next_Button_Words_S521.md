## 2. The fifth chapter's name: reworded, on all seven schools

All seven stories are signed, and all seven name the fifth chapter the same way, so one ruling covers them.

| | Now | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| The chapter's name | Its Most Helpful Forms | What You Can Learn in This School |
| The line where the other chapters carry years | Taught in This School | Today |

So the button reads **Read Chapter Five: What You Can Learn in This School**, and the card's label reads **Next: Chapter Five of Five**.

Why. Kain named "5 its most helpful forms" as vague, and it is: read alone, "Its" points at nothing and "Forms" names nothing a visitor can picture. The chapter holds what the school's courses teach now, each moment naming its course, so the name says that. The name was not made "What This School Teaches", because that is already the heading of another block on the same page, and two things on one page never share a name.

Nothing else in the chapter changes: its moments, their titles, their words and their course links stand as signed. Each story's lede still ends on "the forms of it this school teaches" and stands as signed. The other four chapter names stand: read under the story's own heading and beside their years, "Where It Began", "What Inspired It", "How It Grew" and "What It Is Today" each say what the chapter holds.

## 3. No describing sentence: the moment titles stand
