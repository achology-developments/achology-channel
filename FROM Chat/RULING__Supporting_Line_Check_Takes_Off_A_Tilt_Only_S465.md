## 1. The ruling

**Only a rotation is taken off. A scale or a slide is read as drawn.** This is your choice 1.

The reason. The 24 is the clear space the eye sees between the supporting line and the top of what follows.

- **A rotation** turns a thing about its middle. One corner rises and the other falls by the same amount, and the thing stays where it was laid out. Reading the raised corner reads the look, not the space. So the rotation is taken off before the read.
- **A scale or a slide** moves the whole top edge of the thing, level, nearer to the line or further from it. The eye then sees a different space. So the edge is read where it is drawn.
