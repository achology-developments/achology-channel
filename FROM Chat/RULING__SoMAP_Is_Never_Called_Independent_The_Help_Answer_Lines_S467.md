# SoMAP is never called independent: the Help answer lines

**The ruling.** The word independent, in every form, comes off the Society of Modern Applied Psychology (SoMAP), off Achology's accreditation and verification, and off the pathway. The verification committee is three Master Achologists (Alec Wells, Erika Nadeau, Gabriele Tzeschlock), who are Achology members; they check each member's claim against its evidence and take no part in Achology's commercial operations (REF 1 sections 5.1 and 10.3, as changed 10 November 2026). The signed Certification page says SoMAP reviews every certificate of both kinds before issue.

**What Chat did.** Every HELP__ record in the help-answer folder was read for the stem "independen" (49 records carry it). Lines about other bodies (ANLP, ICF, clinical hypnosis, counselling licensure), about a reader's own independent practice, about Achology's editorial independence from sponsors, or about Achology as an independent provider were left alone. Where the same sentence also called SoMAP "outside Achology" or "an outside check", that phrase went too, because it makes the same claim in other words; four such sentences in records already being changed are included. Where Achology invites the reader to ask whether an accreditor is "independent of the seller", the test now asks whether it "stands apart from" the seller, the wording the signed answer on choosing a mental health course already uses, and which SoMAP truly meets. Each sentence is replaced whole; only the words truth needed were changed.
