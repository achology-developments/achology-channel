## Introduction (under the heading "Live Events Are the Heartbeat of Achology")

Every week, members meet live to practise, reflect and learn together. Here is every kind of event Achology runs, and what happens at each.

## Card lines (written fresh; none taken from the Events page)

1. Skill Development Workshop: A hands-on weekly session where you practise one applied psychology skill with the group, then try it again with what you learned.
2. Principle-Based Reflective Discussion: A senior Achologist opens with questions about one course principle. Then the conversation is yours: how it has shown up in your life.
3. Achologist-Led Community Tutorial: An Achologist leads a live community tutorial on one personal development idea, with room to ask your questions as you go.
4. Live Coaching Practice and Feedback: Practise coaching live while others observe, then hear feedback on what you did and how to make it stronger next time.
5. Achology Code of Ethics Training: Four times a year, members come together to work through the Achology Code of Ethics and what it asks of us in practice.
6. Code of Character and Conduct Training: Every week, members explore the Code of Character and Conduct and what living by it looks like in everyday life.
7. Coffee and Conversation: An informal chat group where members talk about whatever is on their minds, with no agenda and no need to prepare anything.
8. Communication Skills and People Skills: A weekly mentorship group for building the way you listen, speak and deal with people, alongside others working on the same.
9. The Wiser Woman Project: A mentorship group that meets twice a month, built around the Wiser Woman Project, for sharing experience and learning from one another.
10. Office Hours with Karen Ramsay: Bring your questions to Karen Ramsay, one of Achology's founders, in a weekly hour set aside for you and your questions.
11. Mission Driven Mondays: Once a month, start the week by getting clear on what matters most and what you will do about it, with the group beside you.
12. On Becoming a Person: A group that meets every other week to discuss Carl Rogers' On Becoming a Person and what its ideas mean for your own life.
