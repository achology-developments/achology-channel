## Introduction (under the heading "Live Events Are the Heartbeat of Achology")

Every week, members meet live to practise, reflect and learn together. Here is every kind of event Achology runs, and what happens at each.

## Card lines (written fresh; none taken from the Events page)

1. Skill Development Workshop: A hands-on weekly session of 90 minutes where you practise one applied psychology skill with the group, then try it again with what you learned from them.
2. Principle-Based Reflective Discussion: A senior Achologist opens with questions about one course principle. Then the conversation is yours: how it has shown up in your life and how you use it.
3. Achologist-Led Community Tutorial: An Achologist leads a live community tutorial every week on one personal development idea, with room to ask your questions and to talk it through with other members.
4. Live Coaching Practice and Feedback: Practise coaching live while others observe, then hear feedback on what you did and how to make it stronger next time, in a weekly session of 90 minutes with the group.
5. Achology Code of Ethics Training: Four times a year, members come together for an hour to work through the Achology Code of Ethics and what it asks of every one of us in daily practice and in our work.
6. Code of Character and Conduct Training: Every week, members spend an hour exploring the Code of Character and Conduct and what living by it looks like in the middle of everyday life and work.
7. Coffee and Conversation: An informal chat group where members talk about whatever is on their minds, with no agenda and no need to prepare anything. It is held every week for all members.
8. Communication Skills and People Skills: A weekly mentorship group for building the way you listen, speak and deal with people, alongside others who are working on the very same skills as you.
9. The Wiser Woman Project: A mentorship group that meets twice a month, built around the Wiser Woman Project, for sharing what you have lived through and learning from one another.
10. Office Hours with Karen Ramsay: Bring your questions to Karen Ramsay, one of Achology's founders, in a weekly hour set aside for members to ask her anything they need help with in their learning.
11. Mission Driven Mondays: Once a month, start the week by getting clear on what matters most and what you will do about it, with the whole group beside you as you plan your days ahead.
12. On Becoming a Person: A group that meets every other week to discuss Carl Rogers' On Becoming a Person and what its ideas mean for your own life and for the people around you every day.
