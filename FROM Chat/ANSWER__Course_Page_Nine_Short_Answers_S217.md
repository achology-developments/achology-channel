## 1. `q-start`: Is this the right course for me to start with?

Start here if this subject is the thing you came to learn. {course_name} asks for no earlier study, qualification or background, so a complete beginner can begin it today. The more useful test is whether the course answers a question you already have, rather than whether it looks like the most impressive place to start.

Some advanced courses recommend a foundation course first, because their later lessons build on earlier ground. That's advice to help you follow along and never a condition of entry. If you'd rather have the whole field mapped before you specialise, the Diploma Course in Modern Applied Psychology (DiMAP) is the broad foundation, and it sits in all seven schools.

Because access never expires, beginning in the wrong place costs you a detour and nothing more.

## 2. `q-practical`: Will I learn practical skills, or mostly theory?

Practical skills, with enough theory to show you why they work. The teaching is led by principles rather than scripts, because a principle still serves you in a situation no course foresaw, where a memorised procedure tends to fail. Each idea in {course_name} is meant to be tried: on your own habits, in your daily life and with other people.

Knowing a model isn't the same as using it well. Someone can name every habit of skewed thinking that psychology has catalogued and still snap at a partner or take feedback badly, so the learning is built around reflection and practice, not only the videos. The courses are educational, not clinical: none of them teaches diagnosis or treatment.

## 3. `q-school`: Should I buy this course alone or the whole school?

Buy {course_name} on its own if this one subject is what you want. Buy the school if you already know you'll want the courses around it. Every course belongs to at least one of the seven schools, and a school bundle costs between 21% and 26% less than buying its courses and a year's membership separately.

A bundle runs from five courses to 12, always including the Diploma Course in Modern Applied Psychology (DiMAP), and it comes with 12 months of membership in place of the three months a single course carries. Bundles cost from $987 to $1,337. Moving from one course to a bundle later is arranged case by case with the team, and no automatic credit for a course you've already bought is promised, so it pays to choose before you buy.

## 4. `q-hours`: How many hours a week will the course take?

As many as you choose to give it. {course_name} is self-paced with no deadline, so your week sets the pace, not a timetable. At a few hours a week, a Masterclass usually takes weeks and a practitioner course months. Plan from the teaching hours shown on this page, then set aside extra time for the exercises and practice.

The quoted hours are video hours, and the learning happens in what you do between the videos. Watching the whole course in a few days is possible, but it leaves the ideas watched and not learned. Nothing on the platform hurries you along, and nothing chases you if a busy month slows you down.

## 5. `q-time`: How long do I have to finish the course?

As long as you need. Once you've bought {course_name}, it stays in your account with no expiry date and no renewal charge, so you can finish in a month, a year or longer, and come back to any lesson whenever you like. When the course is updated, the new material appears in your account automatically and costs nothing extra.

The one part with an end date is membership, the community around the courses, which comes with the course for three months. If you let it go, the course itself is untouched.

## 6. `q-practise`: Will I get to practise what I learn with other people?

Yes, if you want to. The three months of membership that come with {course_name} open the community's live online events, where members practise skills together, take turns coaching while others watch and give feedback, and talk through real cases. Each practice session runs for 90 minutes, and events are on the calendar Monday to Friday.

You can also offer or take one to one coaching practice with another member through the coaching board, which is where most members' regular practice comes from. None of it is compulsory, but practice is where a course becomes something you can do rather than something you've watched.

## 7. `q-membership`: What happens when my included membership ends?

It carries on unless you stop it. When the three free months that came with your purchase end, membership renews at $34.50 a month, so if you'd rather not continue, cancel in the community platform before the renewal date and nothing more is charged. You can cancel at any time, and whatever you decide, {course_name} stays yours.

If you do cancel, you give up the community: the live events, peer practice, mentorship groups and discussion spaces, and the seven courses that come with membership itself. Anything you bought separately keeps its lifetime access, and you can rejoin whenever you miss the live events.

If you stay, the annual plan at $345 costs less than 12 monthly payments. Membership payments aren't refunded once a period has begun, so put the renewal date in your diary the day you enrol.

## 8. `q-instalments`: Can I pay for the course in monthly instalments?

Yes. At checkout you can pay {course_price} in full or spread it over monthly instalments, with no interest and no surcharge, so what you pay in all is the same {course_price}. The first instalment is taken when you enrol, and your access starts that same day. The checkout shows how many instalments this course offers.

You can pay by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express or Discover) or with Apple Pay. Your three free months of membership come with the course whichever way you pay. Membership itself is billed monthly or annually, so it never goes on an instalment plan.

## 9. `q-refund`: What if the course turns out not to be for me?

Ask within 14 days of buying {course_name} and you'll get a full refund, with no reason needed and no argument over your decision. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with. That gives you two weeks to work through the opening lessons and find out whether the course suits the way you learn.

The guarantee can be used once per course. Refunding the course doesn't end the membership that came with it, so cancel that as well if you don't want it to renew; membership payments themselves aren't refunded.

If you'd like to test the fit before you spend anything, join one of the free live events first, or send your question through the enquiries page.
