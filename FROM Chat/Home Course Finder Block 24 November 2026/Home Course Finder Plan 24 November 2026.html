What it does

A visitor presses as many or as few buttons as they like, in any order. The block shows every Achology product that fits, right there, with a count. All roads lead to the right course: if nothing fits exactly, it shows the nearest ones and says so. It never shows an empty result.

It is the place the hero's button, "Find the Right Course for You", leads to.
