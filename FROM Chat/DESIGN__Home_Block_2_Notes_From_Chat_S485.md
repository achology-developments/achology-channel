## Why it still reads as a template

1. **The drawings are three different icon styles, not one drawing.** A monitor with a play button, two speech-bubble figures, and a figure with a sunburst beside two smaller ones. Each is a stock idea, each is built differently, and the monitor says "video platform", which Achology is not. Under the signed hero, a rich, dark, photographic statement, they look thin and borrowed.
2. **White cards stand on a white band.** DSRD 7 section 3.3a rule 5: "white cards sit on the tint so their edges show". Here only a shadow at 8% marks their edges, so the block looks unfinished at the sides.
3. **Nothing tells the eye the three are one thing.** The heading says "at once" but the layout is three equal, separate boxes. That is the missing design layer.
