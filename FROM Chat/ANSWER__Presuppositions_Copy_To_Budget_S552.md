## Where each fact comes from

- **Bandler and Grinder:** the NLP article and course lessons 003-011 and 004-017. They co-founded NLP and drew up the first presuppositions by modelling Virginia Satir, Fritz Perls and Milton Erickson. The map is not the territory is one of the presuppositions. Their card now points to presupposition three rather than to the courses, because the brief asks every card to name one presupposition. The map is the article's own link to checking your thinking.
- **Rogers:** the person-centred counselling article. Every person carries the resources to grow; the relationship releases them; the 1957 conditions included warm acceptance with no strings and empathic understanding.
- **Ellis:** the Ellis article (upset less by events than by beliefs; the ABC model). REBT no longer appears on the card, so it needs no spelling out. The card names presupposition four, which the Ellis article and the beliefs article both tie to his work.
- **Frankl:** the Man's Search for Meaning article (the choice of how to respond can't be taken from you; the book comes out of his time in the camps). The card now names presupposition five, because the choice of response is the heart of emotional responsibility. Maslow keeps presupposition seven, so no two cards name the same one.
- **Maslow:** the Maslow biography (he studied people who flourish, not only the ill; his late finding that the most mature people served a cause outside themselves).
- **The seven rows:** each row keeps its meaning from its own article.
