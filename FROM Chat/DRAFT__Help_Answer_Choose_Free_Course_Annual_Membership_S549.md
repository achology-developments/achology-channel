#### 0. The stances this answer rests on

**The Achology Stance On Self Responsibility.md**

Every person alone is responsible for whether they take the steps that would improve their life. Nobody can grow on another person's behalf. No course, no practitioner and no certificate can do it for them.

Common view versus Achology's view: the common view is that a free course is a bonus to be collected. Achology's view is that the best course to own is the one the person will actually start, so the choice begins with where the person is now.

# How do I choose my free course with annual Achology membership?

![A person working on a laptop, representing Achology membership and account access](https://achologytest.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/membership-and-access.webp)

So how do you choose your free course? After you join [Achology Membership](/membership/) for the year, you're taken to a page called Choose Your Free Course, where you pick one of nine courses, enter the name and email address you joined with, and Achology adds it to your account within 24 hours. It's yours for life, even if your membership ever ends, because the annual plan includes one course of your choice.

## Who gets a free course?

Members on the annual plan do. You pay for the annual plan once for the year, and the course comes with it, whereas the monthly plan, paid month by month, doesn't include one. If you're weighing the two, the answer on monthly and annual plans, listed under Related questions, sets them side by side. You choose one course, and each of the nine is a single topic explored in depth, so it's a good way to try a subject properly before you decide to take more.

## What are the nine courses about?

All nine come from the School of [Personal Growth and Development](/academy/personal-growth/). Each one takes a single topic and goes deep into it, so none is a quick overview. Each course appears under its full title on the page, so you can read exactly what it's called before you choose. Between them they cover:

- self-belief, emotional intelligence and assertiveness
- authentic confidence, identity and self-esteem
- emotional intelligence and social skills
- clarity, purpose and personal effectiveness
- strategic goal setting and action planning
- communication skills and social intelligence
- focus, self-discipline and productivity
- mental toughness and inner resilience
- a healthy marriage and healthy relationships

## How do you decide between them?

Start from where you are now, because the course you'll actually begin is worth more than the one that sounds impressive. If you doubt yourself, the confidence and self-belief ones are the natural place to start. If conversations with other people are the hard part, look at communication and emotional intelligence.

If you know what you want but keep not doing it, goal setting, or focus and self-discipline, will meet you there. If setbacks flatten you, mental toughness is the one to pick. If the strain is in your closest relationship, choose the one on a healthy marriage and relationships. Growth can't be done for you, and a course only works when you work through it, so choose the one you'd be glad to open tonight.

## What happens after you choose?

You'll get an email confirming your choice. Achology adds each free course to a member's account by hand, so please allow up to 24 hours. Once it's there, it stays yours for life.

If it hasn't appeared after 24 hours, reply to the confirmation email, and Achology will sort it out. If you can't find the Choose Your Free Course page at all, [contact us](/enquiries/) and we'll point you to it. Please don't wait longer than that, because the sooner you ask, the sooner it's put right.
