The Photograph Ask: Three Things to Sign

Your yes covers these three pieces as they read here: the post for the community, the email for members who have left, and one new line in the Privacy Policy. Nothing has been sent or posted.

1. The community post, in your name

May We Show Your Photograph on the New Achology Website?

We're building a new Achology website, and one page on it answers a question people often ask: who is Achology? My answer is that we are. Achology is this community, and that means everyone who has learned here, whether you joined last month or years ago.

So the page will show nothing but the people. It won't be a faculty or a team page. It will be the faces of members, side by side, and a few of them will also appear in a small collage on the home page.

Some of the photographs we'd like to use are ones you've shared in this community, and some were taken at our meetups. This community is private and the website is public, so your photograph won't go on it unless you say yes.

If you're happy to be on the page, press the button below. It takes one click and asks you for nothing else.

Yes, Show My Photograph

If you say nothing, we'll leave your photograph out. If you say yes and later change your mind, email support@achology.com and we'll take it down. You won't need to give a reason.

Thank you for being part of what Achology is.

Kain Ramsay, founder of Achology

2. The email, for members who have left

Subject: May We Show Your Photograph on the New Achology Website?

You were a member of the Achology community, and I'm writing to ask you one thing.

We're building a new Achology website, and one page on it answers the question of who Achology is. My answer is that Achology is its people: everyone who has learned here, including those who have since moved on. So the page will show nothing but members' faces, and a few of them will also appear in a small collage on the home page.

We have a photograph of you from your time in the community. The community was private and the website is public, so your photograph won't go on it unless you say yes.

If you're happy to be on the page, press the button below. It takes one click and asks you for nothing else.

Yes, Show My Photograph

If you'd rather not, you don't need to do anything, and we'll leave your photograph out. If you say yes and later change your mind, email support@achology.com and we'll take it down. You won't need to give a reason.

Thank you for the time you spent learning with us.

Kain Ramsay, founder of Achology

Sent only to former members whose photograph we have.

3. The new line in the Privacy Policy, after "Images and recordings"

Members' photographs on our website: Where a member has told us they agree, we show their photograph on public pages of achology.com. We rely on that member's consent to do this. A member can withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@achology.com, and we will then remove the photograph.

Written in the policy's own formal style, to sit among its other lines.
