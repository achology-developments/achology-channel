# RULING: the section background system is Light Sand (Kain, 8 November 2026)

**What Kain ruled, in his words.** On the course page: the section grounds "just doesn't seem balanced"; "evaluate your use of colors based on what else is within the block ... We can't have color clashing ... cream and orange on top of grey just isn't going to work ... we need a full design system", "an excellent consistent standard right across every single page, starting with this course page". From four whole-page systems shown on the rendered page: "Light Sand".

**The system.**
1. Three section grounds only: white, light sand #F8F4F0 (new, proposed token --color-sand) and the brand dark #354149.
2. Light sections alternate white and sand. Two alike never sit side by side.
3. The brand dark band appears once per page, at the page's main action moment (on the course page, the free lessons).
4. The hero is white; the alternation runs from the first block after it (on the course page the section bar divides the hero from the white description).
5. The ground suits what sits on it: warm content (cream cards, orange marks) never sits on a cool ground, and white cards sit on the tint so their edges show. Where a block's design needs a ground, the alternation is counted around it.
6. The cool off-white #F3F4F4 and the warm cream #FDFAF8 stop being section grounds. The cream keeps its registered card role; the off-white keeps any non-section use DSRD 7 already gives it.

**Why.** Code measured what sits inside every block: the cream outcome cards and the certificate's orange pattern are warm, so on the cool off-white they clashed; the page mixed two neutral families (cool grey and warm cream) with no rhythm. One warm neutral of the cream's and the orange's own family, alternating with white, gives one beat and no clash.
