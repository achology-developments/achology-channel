# RULING and SHIP: Certification page, section 5 becomes From Study to Practice, 0.707.837 (S464)

**Needs from Chat:** a second-reader (Content Standard Part 19) check on the words below, which Code drafted from Kain's account in the sitting. Code also asks Chat to confirm two facts. First, PALS: REF 1 spells it "Peer-Peer Applied Learning Sessions", where the live site says "Peer-Peer Assisted". Second, "endorsed by SoMAP" for the Certificate of Achievement, taken from the old site's copy.

**Kain's rulings, in order.**
- He asked for more explanation in section 5: "we can afford for this section to be a bit more wordy, a bit more explanatory ... not just another table".
- He then gave his account of autonomous learning: "at Achology we believe in autonomous learning ... there is not a faculty ... not a conventional training organisation ... workbooks, worksheets within every single course. Some courses contain in excess of 100 ... learning is the sole responsibility of the student". Reference accreditation. The three live sessions in membership, which comes with every course.
- "I really only want there to be one heading ... and subheading for this block."
- On the simplified block: "it just feels empty ... what is the purpose of this block?" Purpose agreed: the road from study to practice, each certificate placed where it is earned. This answers section 4's last line, that competence grows only when a learner chooses to practise.
- "completely hands off. We're not here to steer, to coerce, to drive, to motivate, to inspire ... like going into an all-you-can-eat buffet ... 28 courses, 7 schools ... we can't make any guarantees in terms of learning outcomes."
- He picked Two Panels. "We need kind of synchronicity": the panels now mirror each other line for line. Then: "apply the same background design to the two footers ... as ... the recognises study, recognises practice cards", for consistency. "Yes, build it."

**The words placed** (template-certification.php, $ach_road and section 5).
- Heading: From Study to Practice. Supporting line: "We provide the teaching and the materials. What you study, how far you take it and what you apply are yours to choose."
- Opening: "Think of Achology as an open table rather than a classroom: 28 courses across seven schools, covering psychology, mindset, productivity, relationships, communication, goal setting and more. Nobody steers you. Because only you can apply what you learn, we don't promise outcomes. Everything you need to learn is there."
- Panel 1, Study on Your Own: "Work through any course at your own pace. Nobody sets your hours or checks in on you."
  - Lessons: "Every theory, model and practice the course teaches, set out in order".
  - Workbooks and Worksheets: "More than 100 in some courses, to test each idea on yourself".
  - Lifetime Access: "Every lesson stays yours for life, to return to whenever you need". This is from the course page's signed step.
  - Foot: Certificate of Course Completion, "Finish every lesson. Included in the price."
- Panel 2, Practise with Others: "Membership comes with every course. Use it to practise what you've learned, if you choose."
  - VALTS: "Virtual Achologist Led Training Sessions: groups led by senior members".
  - PALS: "Peer-Peer Applied Learning Sessions: one-to-one practice with a member".
  - CIPS: "Competency Improvement Practice Sessions: coaching people outside Achology".
  - Foot: Certificate of Achievement, "Six group sessions. Endorsed by SoMAP."
- Close: the How Accreditation Works link.
