**The fifteen answers, Code's words; every fact from REF 1 sections 3.2 and 3.3 and the signed Help answers valts-achology, pals-earn-them and cips-when-need-them.**

VALTS, Virtual Achologist Led Training Sessions:
1. A live 90-minute group session where you practise a skill or principle from a course, with feedback as you work.
2. A small group of members, hosted by a senior member.
3. Attend and take part. Each session you attend earns you one VALTS credit.
4. Booked from the events calendar. There are six types, from skill workshops to live coaching.
5. From the start. Six sessions, across two types, are part of reaching Achologist.

PALS, Peer-Peer Applied Learning Sessions:
1. One-to-one coaching practice between members. You're coached first, so you know the client's chair before you coach.
2. One other member at a time, as client or as coach.
3. Be coached, then coach. Each session, received or delivered, is counted.
4. Offered and claimed on the Coaching Board. Some are given live at group events instead.
5. From the start as a client. You begin coaching other members at Achologist Adept.

CIPS, Competency Improvement Practice Sessions:
1. Free one-to-one coaching you give to someone outside Achology: a real person with a real challenge or goal.
2. One person at a time, from outside the community.
3. Deliver the session. Each one is a CIPS credit, up to six with one person.
4. Offered on the Get Free Coaching board, where people outside Achology apply for a session.
5. Not until Senior Achologist I, once you've coached and been coached by members.
