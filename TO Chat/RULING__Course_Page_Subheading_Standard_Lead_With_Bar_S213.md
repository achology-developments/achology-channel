# RULING: every block's subheading is Lead with Bar (Kain, 8 November 2026)

**What Kain ruled, in his words.** On the course page he found every block's subheading "exactly the same font size and weight as the main body text", asked for one heading and one subheading in every block, "a website-wide standard", and chose from four styles shown on the rendered page, each applied to all nine subheadings at once: "Lead with Bar".

**The standard.** A block's supporting line (the one line under a block heading, DSRD 7 section 3.3 rules 1 to 5) is set in the register's Lead Paragraph: Mulish 18px, weight 500, line height 1.55, brand dark #354149, with a 2px orange edge (--color-orange) on its left and 16px (--sp-md) between the edge and the words. On a dark band the line keeps that band's own light text colour; the edge and the size are the same. The block heading above it stays Como 24/600.

**Why.** At the body's own size and weight the supporting line could not be told from a paragraph; the register's Lead row already says why it exists ("deliberately larger than body so the page announces what it is"). The orange edge is the one What You Will Learn already carried (course page pick 213, Kain: "mirror the rest of the page"), now on every block rather than two of nine.

**Where it stands.** Built and checked on the course page preview only (previews/course-page-s208, PICKS 220 and 221), not on the build site. No other page is touched: section 3.3 currently names no type for the supporting line, so this fills that gap, and other pages follow only under a sweep brief.

**Also from the same sitting, for DSRD 9's course page layout:** What You Will Learn is now two bands, the description on white and "By the End of This Course" with the outcome cards on off-white, the latter's heading at the section size (pick 220); How the Course Works and the Certificate are one block (picks 208 to 219), with Kain's own copy.
