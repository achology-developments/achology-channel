## 2. What changed, and why

The eight cards' course lists (the seven schools and the Access All Areas Pass) snapped open and shut, because a native disclosure has no in-between. Each list now eases open and shut, a third of a second long, in the Smooth Lift's ease (Kain, S209), so a visitor sees where the list came from.

His S128 ruling is untouched: the disclosure stays native, so the browser owns open and shut and the keyboard and a screen reader meet the same control. pricing.js only runs the list's height and opacity between the two states. A visitor who asks their device for less motion, or has no script, gets the snap as before. No word, figure, size or space changes, and pricing.css is untouched by this pick.
