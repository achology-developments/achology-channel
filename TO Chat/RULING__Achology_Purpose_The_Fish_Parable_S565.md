**How it came.** Kain rejected the assembled Home page for having no vision, then gave his own, "plainly", as a parable. His words, with only repeats cut:

1. "Give a man a fish, he will eat for today. Our personal growth and development courses give people that fish."
2. "Teach a person how to fish for themselves ... and they eat for a lifetime. Those are our practitioner courses." What that means to him: "all of the knowledge and insights and tools and soft skills that they need to manage and regulate and improve themselves and develop a relationship with truth, so they can objectively evaluate themselves and hold themselves to account for the rest of their lives, their mindset, their perspectives, their attitudes, their behaviors, which is all that they're fundamentally responsible for".
3. "Teach people how to fish, and together, collectively, we end world hunger." Once a person can do that for themselves, "they become naturally equipped to start positively influencing others through coaching, mentorship, hosting workshops, facilitating discussion groups, or even training events".

**Where the third stage happens.** "It is only through the academy, the community portal, and the community learning environment that people can actively participate in workshops and events and really plug into the heart of the community, connect with the community leadership team, where they can start to get experience in coaching people, mentoring people, and also facilitating and hosting their own workshops and events within the safety of a private training environment."

**What makes it different.** The community "is not overseen by a top-down management or a faculty. Instead, it presents an open floor classroom where any student can step up and talk about the topics or the ideas that they're learning, for the purpose of learning deeper within themselves or sharing what they've learned or their insights with others."

**Who it is for.** "Anyone can come from any walk of life and enroll on a course ... for the purpose of getting a fish, or for the purpose of learning how to fish for themselves."

**The contrast he wants the Home page to carry (same sitting).** He named Mindvalley as the opposite: a homepage "plastered with guru culture ... men and women who have self-appointed themselves to the position of guru, industry-level expert". "That is not what Achology is, and it's not what Achology values. The glass ceiling within Mindvalley are the instructors and the self-proclaimed gurus. Within Achology, there is no glass ceiling, because any member can literally study, take courses, participate in workshops and events, all hosted by members and other learners within the learning community. There is not a faculty ... There's no restriction to how far anyone can grow." A member can host workshops, facilitate events, "eventually even hosting their own mentorship group if they have something valuable to mentor other community members in". The purpose: "not just becoming confident in the training materials, but actually becoming confident in teaching and training and facilitating others". Read by Code on 24 November 2026: Mindvalley's own pages say "Learn from the world's best", "Get coached by the world's top experts" and "Our experts are living legends", show 24 named teachers with photographs, and show members only as testimonials and a social network. The belief on the wider industry still rules: no provider is named on the page.

**The learner Achology is for (same sitting).** Kain set Achology against guru brands in general, the large platform above and the well-known personal brands built round one named expert: brands with "a strong sales team", whose ideal learner is "the disempowered learner who does not want to take responsibility for driving their own learning ... They want to remain ... subjected to a guru or a guru brand or an expert for the rest of their life and essentially get told what to do." "Achology doesn't have a strong sales team behind it. What it does have instead is a community infrastructure that is not going to hinder anyone from learning as fast or progressing as far as what they independently want to go." "Achology serves a very different type of learner. That is the learner who actually wants to learn, who actually wants to become competent, who wants to become an expert, and who ... aspires to become a positive role model in the lives of other people. Be this through parenting ... management ... leadership ... social influence, online, offline, a social influencer, a charity leader, a mentor, a coach, or even a therapist." No brand and no person is named on any page; the contrast is carried by what Achology shows, not by what it says about anyone else.

**The second parable: the man in the hole (same sitting).** Kain told it to show "the layer of competency that our senior Master Achologists become adept in". A man falls into a hole and cannot get out. A doctor passes, writes a prescription, throws it in and says to shout next week if symptoms persist. A minister passes, says a prayer, forgives him and walks on. A counsellor passes, sits on the edge and asks how he feels about being in the hole and about his mother and father, until his watch beeps and he says to book again next week. Then a fourth man looks in, says "John, how'd you get in that hole?" and jumps in with him. "What are you doing? ... I've been in this hole all day. I don't know how to get out." "Yeah, I know. But listen, I've been in this hole before, and I know how to get out."

What he draws from it, in his words: members are encouraged "not to become guru brands, but to become experts in the paths that they've already walked, be that parenting, leadership, overcoming hardship, building a business, whatever. We encourage all of our members to only offer wisdom, insights, coaching, support, help, mentorship in areas that they actually already have expertise and experience in. Because we understand one thing, and this is that authority isn't a given thing. No one can give any other human being any level or depth of authority. Authority comes from our understanding of the experiences that we've already been through."
