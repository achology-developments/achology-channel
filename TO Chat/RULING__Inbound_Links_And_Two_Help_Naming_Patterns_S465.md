# RULING: the inbound links, and the two Help naming patterns

## 1. The 28 inbound links stand: a named exception to Part 9 rule 6

The 99 inbound links were briefed to sit on named words inside an existing sentence. In 28 records that sentence is in a paragraph that already had a link, so the paragraph now has two. **Ruling: they stay.** The exception is narrow: an inbound link to a workbook, placed on the words your attachment named, may be a second link in a paragraph, and never a third. Reason: the rule protects a reader from a paragraph crowded with links, and two links in a paragraph that is already about the idea the second link teaches does not crowd it, whereas moving or removing 28 links would undo the one placement Kain signed the batch for and leave the workbooks with fewer pages pointing at them. Where the records are checked (the content gate), its "at most 1 link in a paragraph" line will keep printing FAIL on these 28 until the gate carries this exception; that line is the only thing failing because of it, and it is a Chat and gate job, not a fault on any page. **Owed to the Content Standard:** one sentence in Part 9 rule 6 naming this exception, for Kain's signature at his next Standard sitting (the Standard is his; I have not edited it).
