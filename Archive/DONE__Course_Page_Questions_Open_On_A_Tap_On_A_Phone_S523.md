# Course page questions on a phone: each question opens on a tap (pick 326, 0.708.097)

**What changed.** On every course page, under 768 wide, the nine answer cards of Questions People Ask Before They Enrol no longer stand one under another with every answer showing. Each card shows its question alone, with a chevron. A tap on the card or the chevron opens the answer and its Read the full answer link, and the one open before it closes. The block stands about 1,260 tall where it stood about 2,670. No word and no link was changed. From 768 up nothing changes.

**Why.** Kain, on his walk of the Diploma page, 16 November 2026. Four ways were shown in the one Change Viewer (round 11 of the course outcome cue preview folder in the theme's previews). He chose the first: "Questions open in a tap, and I like it", and, once it opened on a tap for him, "Yes, build it".

**A fault of mine on the way, mended.** In the preview the questions did not open on a tap in his Safari. The preview read the window's width once as the page loaded, and the viewer's frame takes the phone's width a moment later. Mended in the preview, and the build never reads the width at load.

**Where.** The theme's course page stylesheet and script, the questions block only. The script adds the chevron button and marks the wall, so without the script every answer shows as before. Recorded as pick 326 in the course page picks record.

**Proof.** Measured in WebKit at 360, 390, 767, 768, 1024 and 1440 on the Diploma and the Mental Health Practitioner Diploma: a tap on a question, a press on the chevron and Enter on the chevron each open and close; one open at a time; no card wider than its screen; no script error; the open card's link goes to its Help answer. On the build site the same presses were made at 390, 768 and 1440. Not tried by hand on a real phone.
